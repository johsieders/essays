Below I work through the points by theme, accepting or refuting each. Where three reviewers raise the same point, I treat it once.

## Factual corrections

**Sperrklausel (A).** Accept. The text is wrong: there was a 1 % threshold from the start, not none. Fix: "...mit einer extrem niedrigen Sperrklausel (1 %), die kaum als Hürde wirkte..." This actually strengthens my point — even with a threshold, fragmentation was extreme.

**Entschädigung der Mizrachim (A, B, C).** Accept, important. The sentence conflates two different things. The Mizrachim received Israeli citizenship and integration assistance; they did *not* receive compensation from their countries of origin for confiscated property — which to this day fuels the "Jewish refugee" claims. Fix both occurrences: "...während die Mizrachim die israelische Staatsbürgerschaft und Eingliederungshilfe erhielten — wenn auch keine Entschädigung für ihr in den Herkunftsländern verlorenes Eigentum —, blieben die Palästinenser staatenlos und ohne Kompensation." The corrected version makes the asymmetry *cleaner*, not weaker: the relevant contrast is citizenship/statelessness, not compensation.

**Anerkennung Jordaniens (A, B).** Accept with caution. Both reviewers are right that the standard "Britain and Pakistan" claim is shakier than usually stated; Pakistan's recognition is poorly documented and Britain's was effectively de facto, excluding East Jerusalem. Fix: "...ein Schritt, der international fast einhellig abgelehnt wurde; nur Großbritannien erkannte ihn faktisch an, und auch dies nicht für Ostjerusalem." I'll drop Pakistan rather than assert something I can't source cleanly. I will *not* add Iraq's alleged de facto recognition (A) — that is at least as contested as Pakistan, so adding it trades one uncertain claim for another.

**"Elf Stunden" / "elf Minuten" (B).** Partially accept. The "eleven minutes" for Truman's recognition is correct and worth keeping — it is precise and telling. The "eleven hours" is a rhetorical mirror that overstates precision; the invasion began overnight on 14/15 May. Fix the second only: "Wenige Stunden später, in der Nacht zum 15. Mai, griffen die Armeen... an." Keep the deliberate "elf Minuten" — Truman's haste is a documented fact and the asymmetry with the slower invasion is the point.

**Iran unter "arabischen Ländern" (B).** Accept — a plain inconsistency. Iran is not Arab. Fix: "...aus arabischen und weiteren mehrheitlich muslimischen Ländern (Irak, Jemen, Marokko, Ägypten, Libyen, Tunesien, Syrien sowie dem Iran)."

**Suez: Umfang der Besetzung (C).** Accept. The text says Israel reached the canal but omits that it occupied the *entire Sinai*, which matters for 1967. Fix: "Israel schlug am 29. Oktober ein, besetzte binnen weniger Tage die gesamte Sinaihalbinsel und erreichte den Suezkanal." Reviewer C's note that control was short-lived (10 days) is already implied by the forced withdrawal; I won't belabour it.

**MiG-21 "erbeutet" (B).** Accept — good catch, and the kind of error that annoys readers who know the story. The MiG-21 came via the Iraqi defector Munir Redfa (Operation Diamond, 1966), not capture in battle. Fix: "...den nachrichtendienstlichen Wert Israels (etwa die 1966 durch einen irakischen Überläufer erlangte MiG-21, geteilte Geheimdienstinformationen)." I'll keep "erbeutete sowjetische Waffen" as a general category since plenty *was* captured in 1956 and 1967, but not as the MiG example.

**Mizrachim-Zahl 850.000 (C).** Refute, mostly. 850.000 is the widely cited mid-range figure (the spread is roughly 650.000–900.000 depending on cut-off year and whether Iran is included). I already write "ca. 850.000" and the surrounding text stresses that the comparison with the Nakba is about order of magnitude, not exactness. C's claim that "the lower bound is more plausible (Shohat)" is itself a contested partisan position; Shohat has political reasons to minimise. I'll keep the number but add "(Schätzungen schwanken je nach Stichjahr zwischen rund 650.000 und 900.000)."

**Eichmann-Prozess "vom verdrängten Hintergrund zur zentralen Grundlage" (C).** Partially accept. C is right that the suppression was primarily *public/rhetorical*, not a total absence of memory (Yad Vashem 1953, kibbutz commemoration). My sentence overstates. Fix: "Der Eichmann-Prozess verschob den Holocaust von einem öffentlich eher beschwiegenen Thema ins Zentrum der israelischen Identität." That preserves the real shift without claiming the memory had been absent.

**Wasserprojekt-Detail (C).** Accept as optional sharpening. Adding the Banias diversion (1964) and the Israeli strikes (1965) costs one clause and gains concreteness: "Syrien begann 1964, den Banias-Zufluss umzuleiten, was zu israelischen Militärschlägen führte." Worth doing.

## Logical points

**"Nicht zurückgelassen" (A, B).** Accept — this is a genuine error, not a stylistic quibble. The phrase is sense-inverted. Fix: "Die palästinensischen Flüchtlinge wurden in den meisten Aufnahmeländern weder eingebürgert noch integriert; viele leben bis heute in Lagern (→ Kapitel 9)."

**Bevölkerungsaustausch — category error (A).** Accept, and I like this. A's point is the sharpest single suggestion in the three reviews: the "exchange" argument constructs collective subjects and nets individual wrongs against each other on a collective ledger. I'll add: "Der eigentliche Fehler liegt tiefer: Das Argument saldiert individuelles Unrecht auf kollektiver Ebene. Dass ein Jude aus Bagdad eine Wohnung in Haifa erhielt, macht das Unrecht an einem vertriebenen Bauern aus Jaffa nicht kleiner — es sind verschiedene Menschen." This is a first-principles correction, exactly the kind I prefer over moralising.

**Juristische Asymmetrie / Res. 194 (C).** Partially accept. Worth one sentence, but I'll keep it factual and avoid implying Res. 194 is self-executing law (it is a non-binding General Assembly resolution — calling it "geltendes Völkerrecht," as C's draft does, overstates). Fix: "Hinzu kommt eine rechtliche Asymmetrie: Für die Palästinenser existiert mit UN-Resolution 194 ein — wenn auch unverbindlicher und bis heute strittiger — Bezugspunkt für ein Rückkehrrecht; für die Mizrachim gab es keinen vergleichbaren Anspruch."

**Suez-Schluss: kein "nie allein" (B).** Accept. B is right that my Suez lesson, stated baldly, is contradicted by 1967. Fix: "Israel zog die Konsequenz: Ein Alleingang ohne mindestens stillschweigende amerikanische Duldung ist teuer — Suez wurde zum warnenden Präzedenzfall." That is consistent with the Johnson section ("will not be alone unless it decides to go alone").

**Aufhebung der Militärverwaltung — Motive (C).** Accept as a small addition. Naming the drivers (domestic/international pressure, economic integration) is cheap and improves the analysis. One caveat: C's "Taqwa-Bewegung" appears to be a confused reference — I can't identify an Arab-communist movement by that name; the relevant actor was the Communist Party (Maki) and Arab labour pressure. I'll write the motives without inventing a movement: "Eshkol reagierte auf internationalen Druck, innenpolitische Kritik und die fortschreitende wirtschaftliche Einbindung der arabischen Bürger."

## Style and tone

**Superlative: "gigantisch" (A, B), "aus dem Nichts" ×3 (B).** Accept. These are exactly the embellishments I try to avoid. "gigantisch" → "enorm" / "großes Infrastrukturprojekt." For "aus dem Nichts": keep it once, as a deliberate triad-closing pointe, and replace the others. Actually the threefold repetition in the conclusion is intended as anaphora — but B is right that it tips into the postcard-prose I warn against. I'll keep one ("ein Staat aus dem Nichts") and recast the rest.

**Notfriedhöfe doppelt (A, B, C).** Accept — plain duplication within Chapter 9, an editing slip. Delete the second occurrence.

**Arabische Zerrissenheit doppelt (A).** Accept. The two passages in Chapter 1 say the same thing. Merge: keep the first ("schlecht koordiniert, politisch zerstritten, ohne gemeinsames Kommando") and cut the redundant second sentence, folding only the Czech-arms point forward.

**"systematisch" → "strukturell" (A).** Accept. A and C converge here and are right: "systematisch" implies deliberate state-planned racism; the historiography (Segev, Shohat) describes structural paternalism plus resource scarcity. "Strukturell" is more accurate and, being a weaker claim, harder to attack.

**"herablassend" (C).** Partially refute. C wants "in ein hierarchisches Integrationsmodell gezwungen." But "herablassend" is not merely subjective venting — condescension is precisely what the sources document (the "primitive" labelling, the cultural devaluation I describe right after). I'll keep "herablassend" in the conclusion because it is earned by the evidence in §2, but I'll adopt C's more analytical framing *inside* §2 where the mechanism is described.

**"vollständiger Erfolg" / "verheerend" (B).** Accept. "Militärisch: klarer Erfolg." / "Die politische Reaktion war ein schwerer Rückschlag." Less colour, more precision.

**"zynischer Zusammenhang" (B, C).** Refute. The dry irony here is deliberate and, I think, defensible: a recession ending because a war triggered a boom *is* a grim coincidence worth flagging. But I'll soften "zynisch" to "bitterer Zusammenhang," which carries the observation without the authorial editorialising C objects to. C's alternative (captured Sinai oilfields, US aid) is a separate causal claim and partly anachronistic for 1967–68; I won't adopt it as the explanation.

**"David gegen Goliath" im Schluss (C).** Refute. C calls the metaphor "abgenutzt." It is the title and organising image of the chapter; the final line ("aus David wurde Goliath") is the deliberate payoff of a frame I set up and then dismantle — exactly the kind of kept promise I value in structure. Removing it to avoid cliché would discard the chapter's spine. Keep.

**uvulares R — Monolithie (B).** Accept as a half-clause. Fair. Fix: "uvulares R (verbreitet, aber regional und sozial variabel)."

**Abschnitt 8 additiv; Sprache mit Hierarchie verknüpfen (C).** Accept partially. The link between the Hebrew/Yiddish suppression and the Mizrachi/Arabic suppression is genuinely illuminating and currently only implicit. I'll add one bridging sentence connecting the devaluation of Yiddish ("Diaspora-Relikt") to the parallel devaluation of Arabic-Jewish culture. I won't restructure the whole section — it reads as a deliberate change of register (culture/language as relief from dense political history), which Reviewer A explicitly praised.

## Bias / framing

**"Jüdische Lobby in den USA" (B, C).** Accept. The phrase is loaded and imprecise. Fix: "pro-israelische Organisationen und Unterstützung im Kongress." C is also right that the prime driver of the balance doctrine was Soviet arming of Egypt/Syria (from 1955), not domestic lobbying — I'll reorder so the Cold War logic leads and the domestic factor follows.

**"Gleichgewichtsprinzip wurde zur Doktrin" (B).** Accept, minor. "...prägte zunehmend die amerikanische Politik" rather than asserting a formal doctrine.

**"zionistische Agenten beschleunigten Auswanderung" (B, C).** Accept — this is the most important bias point. Stated as fact, it is irresponsible; this is genuinely contested terrain (the Baghdad bombings of 1950–51 and the long-running accusation against Zionist underground networks remain unresolved). I'll mark it clearly as a contested thesis: "Umstritten ist die These, zionistische Untergrundnetzwerke hätten — etwa im Irak 1950/51 — die Auswanderung durch Verunsicherung beschleunigt; der Forschungsstand ist nicht abschließend. Treibende Kraft war in jedem Fall die staatliche Repression." Both reviewers are right; I will not present it as established.

**"Herut war die Partei der Mizrachim" (B).** Accept. Overstated for the 1950s/60s; the wholesale shift culminated in 1977 (which I describe correctly elsewhere — so the §6 phrasing contradicts my own §2). Fix: "...gewann wachsenden Rückhalt unter den Mizrachim, politisch durchschlagend ab 1977."

**Histadrut 1959 (B).** Accept the hedge. "...öffnete sich erst gegen Ende der 1950er Jahre für arabische Mitglieder."

**"Die westliche Welt applaudierte" / "letzter Moment ungebrochener Sympathie" (B).** Accept the softening. "Große Teile des Westens applaudierten." And "...weitgehend ungebrochener westlicher Sympathie." Reasonable; absolute claims invite easy counterexamples.

**Palästinensische Agency (C).** Partially accept. C is right that the Palestinians appear mainly as objects. But this chapter is explicitly about *Israel 1948–67*, and I twice signpost a dedicated treatment (→ Kapitel 9). I'll add one sentence acknowledging the dual responsibility for the camps' permanence (Israeli refusal of return *and* Arab states' refusal to naturalise), which is accurate and avoids the one-sided "Israel-only" framing — but I won't import a full Palestinian-narrative section that belongs in Chapter 9.

## Additions I decline

- **Saudi/Yemeni contingents, ALA irregulars (B):** true but a level of detail that would clutter the David-Goliath argument; the existing "schlecht koordiniert" already covers it.
- **No Jewish access to the Western Wall 1948–67 (B):** accept this one actually — it's one vivid clause and sharpens the Jordanian-annexation passage: "Juden hatten in dieser Zeit keinen Zugang zur Klagemauer."
- **Histadrut industrial share, Solel Boneh, state-socialist model (C):** worth a single sentence in §5, since I mention Mapai's control of the Histadrut in §6 without explaining the economic model. I'll add one line; I won't expand to Patinkin-level detail.
- **Dimona/France 1960 secret reactor deal (C):** accept one clause in §7, since it explains the Kennedy pressure I already mention.

## Summary of changes I will make

Accept and fix: Sperrklausel; Entschädigung (both spots); Jordanian recognition; "elf Stunden"; Iran; Suez/Sinai; MiG-21; Eichmann phrasing; "nicht zurückgelassen"; category-error sentence; Res. 194 asymmetry; Suez lesson; military-administration motives; "gigantisch"/"aus dem Nichts"; Notfriedhöfe duplication; Arab-disunity duplication; "systematisch"→"strukturell"; "vollständiger Erfolg"/"verheerend"; loaded "jüdische Lobby"; balance-doctrine wording; the zionist-agents thesis (flag as contested); "Herut/Mizrachim"; Histadrut hedge; "westliche Welt"; uvular R; Yiddish/Arabic bridge; Western Wall clause; one line each on the economic model and the France/Dimona deal; one line on dual responsibility for the camps.

Refute or only soften: Mizrachim total (keep 850.000 with range); "herablassend" (keep in conclusion, reframe mechanism in §2); "zynisch" (soften to "bitter," reject C's alternative cause); "David gegen Goliath" (keep — structural spine); full Palestinian-agency section (defer to Chapter 9); "elf Minuten" (keep).

The net effect tightens the facts and removes three or four embellishments without surrendering the chapter's central frame. Reviewer A's category-error point is the single most valuable suggestion; the MiG-21 and "nicht zurückgelassen" catches are the most embarrassing to have left in.
