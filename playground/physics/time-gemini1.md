# Summary of Time and Calendar (Gemini) 
Special Relativity: From Kinematics to Minkowski Geometry

## 1. Proper Time and the Twin Paradox

The relationship between coordinate time $t$ (measured by an inertial observer H) and proper time $\tau$ (measured by a traveling observer K) derives from the invariant spacetime interval. In natural units ($c=1$), the accumulated wristwatch time for K moving with coordinate velocity $v(t)$ is:


$$\tau(T) = \int_0^T \sqrt{1 - v(t)^2} dt$$


This path integral breaks the symmetry of the Twin Paradox: the inertial twin traces a straight worldline (maximizing proper time), while the traveling twin traces a curved worldline, guaranteeing $\tau(T) < T$.

## 2. Proper Velocity and Rapidity

Replacing coordinate time $t$ with proper time $\tau$ defines proper velocity $u$, which scales linearly with momentum and eliminates the cosmic speed limit for the traveler:


$$u = \frac{dx}{d\tau} = \gamma v$$


For constant proper acceleration $\alpha$, the kinematics are dictated by rapidity $\theta = \alpha \tau$. The coordinate and proper velocities map directly to hyperbolic functions:


$$v = \tanh(\theta), \quad \gamma = \cosh(\theta), \quad u = \sinh(\theta)$$


The spatial proper force per unit mass evaluates to $du/d\tau = \gamma^4 a$, which is an unphysical coordinate projection of the true invariant proper acceleration measured by an onboard instrument ($\alpha = \gamma^3 a$).

## 3. Four-Vectors and Invariant Mass

A contravariant four-vector $A^\mu$ transforms under the Lorentz matrix $\Lambda$. Its covariant dual $A_\mu$ (a one-form) is generated by lowering the index via the Minkowski metric $\eta_{\mu\nu}$:


$$A_\mu = \eta_{\mu\nu} A^\nu$$


The four-momentum neatly bundles relativistic energy $E$ and spatial momentum $\vec{p}$:


$$P^\mu = m U^\mu = (E, \vec{p}) = (m\gamma, m\gamma \vec{v})$$


Contracting this vector yields its invariant scalar square, which is simply the particle's rest mass. This proves mass is a fundamental geometric magnitude, rendering the historical concept of "relativistic mass" unnecessary:


$$P \cdot P = P_\mu P^\mu = E^2 - \vert{}\vec{p}\vert{}^2 = m^2$$

## 4. Geometric Derivation of the Lorentz Transformation

Stripped of ad-hoc thought experiments, Special Relativity is simply the geometry of a flat manifold that preserves the Minkowski metric $\eta = \text{diag}(1, -1)$. A linear transformation matrix $\Lambda$ is valid if and only if:


$$\Lambda^T \eta \Lambda = \eta$$


Constrained by this hyperbolic geometry, the matrix natively resolves into the Lorentz boost, proving that time dilation and length contraction are merely geometric projections:


$$\Lambda = \begin{pmatrix} \cosh\theta & \sinh\theta \\ \sinh\theta & \cosh\theta \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \gamma & -v\gamma \\ -v\gamma & \gamma \end{pmatrix}$$
