# Cryptoanalysis


What separates a secret broken in minutes from one that guards the global economy? From the historical Enigma machine to modern AES encryption and quantum threats, cryptology is an ongoing battle between human ingenuity and mathematical limits. Discover how hard problems like prime factorization and discrete logarithms shield your digital world, and explore what happens when quantum computing rewrites the rules.
