## 1. Historical corrections

**"Palästina" / Hadrian (A, B).** Accept. Hadrian reactivated an existing name; *Palaistine* is in Herodot (5th c. BCE) for the coastal region. Fix:

> Der Name *Palaistine* findet sich schon bei Herodot für die Küstenregion; der römische Kaiser Hadrian benannte 135 CE die Provinz Judäa in *Syria Palaestina* um – wohl nach den Philistern, und mit der erkennbaren Absicht, den jüdischen Bezug nach dem Bar-Kochba-Aufstand zu tilgen.

The "wohl" handles B's point that the Philister etymology is not fully secured.

**Demography, 5 million (A, B, C).** Accept the range, partly. The Baron-derived figure of 5–8 million is contested; Frier and McEvedy land near 2–4 million. I will write "von vielleicht 3 bis 4 Millionen" and add that the decline owes as much to assimilation and conversion (especially under Islam) as to persecution — C's substantive point, which improves the argument rather than merely hedging it.

**"Fast 1.800 Jahre" (A).** Reject the specific fix, accept the spirit. A wants "über 1.800" because 1948 − 135 = 1813. But the count should arguably start at 70 CE (destruction of the Temple, loss of the cultic centre), which gives 1878, or even at 586 BCE for the first loss of sovereignty. The number is a round rhetorical figure; precision to the year is false precision here. I will keep "rund 1.800 Jahre" and drop "fast." On B's Himjar/Khazaren point: these were converts or a converted elite, not the continuation of Jewish sovereignty in the relevant sense, but a one-clause footnote costs nothing and removes an attack surface. Accept as footnote.

**Herzl quote (A, B).** Accept. My paraphrase is simply wrong — it garbles the diary entry and drops the "fünf Jahre" clause, which is the witty part (the contrast between Herzl's certainty and the expected ridicule). Use the verified text:

> *"Gründete ich in Basel den Judenstaat, so würde mir heute ein allgemeines Gelächter antworten. Vielleicht in fünf Jahren, jedenfalls in fünfzig wird es jeder einsehen."*

A's wording and B's wording differ slightly; I will check the Tagebuch (3 September 1897) and quote it verbatim rather than trust either reviewer.

**Diaspora beginning, 586 BCE (B).** Accept. The northern deportations (722 BCE) and Elephantine predate Babylon. Fix: "spätestens mit dem babylonischen Exil 586 BCE, mit früheren Anfängen schon nach 722 BCE."

**Aliyot count (B).** Accept. "Fünf große Aliya-Wellen (1882–1948)" is sloppy. The classical five run 1882–1939; Aliyah Bet (1934–48) is the illegal immigration. Fix the parenthesis accordingly.

**Population distribution USA/Israel (B).** Accept. The "Hälfte USA, Drittel Israel" split is roughly a decade out of date; Israel has drawn level or ahead. Replace with "etwa 46 Prozent in Israel, 39 Prozent in den USA, der Rest weltweit" — and I will cite the source (Pew / DellaPergola) since these figures move.

**Sechstagekrieg (B).** Accept. "der Sieg der Sechstagekrieg" → "der Sieg im Sechstagekrieg." Plain typo.

## 2. Nobel numbers

**"965 Nobelpreise" vs. Preisträger (B).** Accept. These are individuals, not prizes. Reformulate: "Seit 1901 wurden rund 1.000 Personen mit dem Nobelpreis ausgezeichnet."

**220 vs. ~200 (A, B, C).** Partially accept. The count is definition-dependent (full vs. partial Jewish ancestry, self-identification). C cites JInfo at ~200; my 220 is at the inclusive end. The honest move is to give a range and name the definitional problem, consistent with the chapter's own promise to state uncertainty:

> Je nach Definition (volle oder teilweise jüdische Herkunft) zählt man etwa 200 bis 215 Personen, rund 20 Prozent aller Preisträger.

I will not pretend to a precision the data do not support. "110-mal höher" then becomes "rund hundertmal" — a back-of-envelope figure, flagged as such.

**Monocausal "Exzellenz" explanation (B, C).** Accept, with caution. The current list (Textkultur, späte Emanzipation, Vertreibung, Konzentration) is good but reads as if complete. I will add: urbanisation and occupational structure, the Haskala's push into secular education, selection effects from migration (the emigrating physicists were disproportionately the productive ones), and — explicitly — survivorship bias and the definitional ambiguity of "jüdisch." I will *not* adopt C's table entry "20% der Nobelpreise in Wissenschaftskategorien" as a replacement, because it silently narrows the claim; the overall figure and the per-field figures are both worth stating.

## 3. Holocaust → Israel causality

**The "Ohne … kein …" construction (A, B, C — unanimous).** Accept. This is the strongest shared criticism and it is correct. The triple "ohne Holocaust kein Konsens / ohne Vertreibung kein Potenzial / ohne Schock keine Resolution" is rhetorically tidy and analytically false as stated. The legal scaffolding (Balfour 1917, Mandat 1922) predates the Holocaust by decades; the Yishuv built facts on the ground from 1882; and the Holocaust *destroyed* the demographic reservoir it supposedly supplied (B's sharpest point — the dead of Wilna and Warschau did not immigrate). I will rewrite to "beschleunigte" / "radikalisierte den internationalen Konsens" and name the other factors: Mandat, Yishuw-Stärke, britischer Rückzug, Supermachtinteressen 1947, DP-Camps.

One caveat against C's proposed phrasing "unausweichlich – aber nicht unvermeidbar": *unausweichlich* and *unvermeidbar* are synonyms in German, so the antithesis collapses. I will not use it.

**Eurocentrism of "Vertreibungen aus Europa" (B).** Accept. The demographic base of early Israel owes as much to the Mizrahi immigration from Arab and Iranian lands (1948–70) as to European survivors. Omitting it is a real gap and I will add a clause.

## 4. Balance / the rejected-offers list

**1947/1978/2000/2008 list one-sided (A, B, C).** Accept in part, reject in part.

Accept: the list as it stands invites the reading that only one side refused, which violates the book's stated method. I will (a) add the Palestinian rationale — that the offers were perceived as lacking genuine sovereignty (contiguous territory, control of borders and airspace, a viable answer on refugees); and (b) name Israeli failures in the same breath: settlement expansion, the assassination of Rabin, weak implementation of Oslo, the unclear parameters at Camp David 2000, and the 2002 Arab Peace Initiative that Israel did not take up. This is required by "gleiche Ernsthaftigkeit."

Reject the framing that this is mere "bias to be corrected away": the asymmetry of military power is already stated, and adding Israeli failures must not tip into the false equidistance the chapter explicitly disavows. Both happened; both belong.

On B's specific 1978 point: correct, Camp David 1978 was the Egyptian–Israeli treaty, and what the PLO rejected was the autonomy framework. I will mark that distinction or drop 1978, which is the weakest item in the list.

## 7. "Überleben" without territory

**Ignores autonomous institutions / Millet, Kahal (C).** Accept, with a precision. The point is well taken: the survival was not of atomised individuals but of self-governing communities with courts, schools, and fiscal autonomy. I will insert "ohne *souveränes* Territorium" and add a clause on communal self-administration. This actually sharpens the riddle rather than dissolving it: the puzzle is survival without sovereignty, not survival without any institutions.

**"Sprache, Bücher, Feste blieben dieselben" (B).** Accept. Liturgical continuity is real; everyday language and rite were not uniform (Aramaic, Judaeo-Arabic, Yiddish, Ladino; varying Nusach). I will qualify: the liturgical and textual core remained, the vernaculars and local rites varied.

## 9. Structure

**Glossary placement (C).** Reject. C wants the glossary moved ahead of the four riddles "so laymen understand the opening." But the four riddles are written to be read cold — that is their function as a hook (the Russell-on-apricots principle: do not explain before the reader feels the need). Front-loading a glossary kills the opening. The glossary is correctly placed as reference *after* the hook; a reader who needs *Tanach* defined on page 1 can look ahead. I will not bury the lede under definitions.

**Abrupt jump riddles → Neue Historiker (C).** Accept. A one-sentence bridge helps: the riddles pose the questions; the Neue Historiker supply the method for answering the conflict ones. Cheap and clarifying.

**Link 7. Oktober to Kapitel 8/9 (C).** Accept. A forward reference costs nothing and serves the reader.
